Learn more → 03 // section.why Why Work With Me Leadership Experience I've led frontend teams, talked directly with executives about technical direction, and shipped products that thousands of people use every day. Systems Thinking I look at the architecture and the processes, not just the features. Six months from now, your team should be moving faster. That's what I optimize for. Business Alignment I translate architecture decisions into language your CEO cares about. Technical calls and business calls are the same call. Most teams just don't treat them that way. Results That Show Two Shark Tank entrepreneurs hired me. Puppertino hit 1,000+ GitHub stars. MapVX went from 8-second loads to under 1. I let the work speak. 04 // section.engagement Engagement Structure Simple terms, no surprises. I want you to know exactly what you are getting. Discovery & Diagnosis
I spend time with your codebase and your team to understand where things stand. I look at the architecture, talk to the engineers, and put together a clear picture of what is working, what is not, and where to go from there.
Typical duration: 2-4 weeks Implementation Partnership
I embed with your engineers and we build together. Reworking architecture, setting up foundational systems, leading a big initiative. I'm another member of the team for as long as the project runs.
Typical duration: 1-3 months Ongoing Advisory
Regular check-ins, architecture reviews, and someone to think through frontend decisions with. You get a frontend lead you can call on whenever you need one, without the full-time commitment.
Typical duration: 6+ months Start with a conversation If you are not sure which one fits, that is totally fine. We can talk it through and figure it out.
